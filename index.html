    BALANCED, EXPLICITLY

    No empty groups.

    Everyone appears once in each round. Group sizes differ by at most one. Some settings create solo groups; the readout makes those placements visible.

    REPEATS, COUNTED

    One pair, several meetings.

    If two people share a group three times, that is one repeated pair and two repeat meetings. The audit keeps those counts separate.

    YOUR SESSION, YOUR CALL

    A starting point to review.

    The mixer does not know roles, personal needs or who should be kept together or apart. Review the plan before using it. No platform integration or messages are sent.

    Input rules, search method and honest limits

    2–48 participant labels, up to 60 printable UTF-16 units each; exact duplicate labels are rejected after trimming. Labels are case-sensitive and not Unicode-normalized. The source roster is limited to 5,000 text units. Title: 1–80 units. Mix key: 1–40 units. Control/format characters, Unicode line separators and unpaired surrogates are unsupported. Use a suitable display alias if needed.

    Choose 1–12 groups, no more than the participant count, and 1–8 rounds. Each round tries at most 24 seeded shuffled candidates, with up to 80 improving cross-group swap attempts per candidate. The objective first reduces pair meetings already seen in earlier rounds, then discourages concentrating them on heavily repeated pairs. Earlier rounds are not reconsidered. This is a bounded heuristic and does not prove an optimum.

    Stable P01-style IDs follow the ordered nonblank roster. Group labels G1, G2, and so on are not room-capacity assignments; larger groups use earlier labels. Nobody is assigned a role or sent anywhere automatically. Pair-audit filters affect the table only. All exports contain complete accepted data.

    No uploads, analytics or application storage. Save an editable project before closing. Browser session restoration and host infrastructure logs are outside application control. The pseudo-random mix key is for reproducibility, not secure draws or guarantees of demographic fairness.
